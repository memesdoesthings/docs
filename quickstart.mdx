This page walks you through the essential steps to start using Cloudy: opening your instance, browsing the web through the proxy, and discovering the games and apps library. By the end, you'll know your way around the core interface and be ready to explore everything Cloudy has to offer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Cloudy">
    Navigate to your Cloudy instance URL in any browser. You'll land on the Cloudy home page, which greets you with a central search bar and clean, minimal interface. If you're running Cloudy locally, the default address is `http://localhost:8080`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the Web">
    Type a full URL (for example, `https://example.com`) or a plain search term into the search bar and press **Enter**. Cloudy routes your request through its proxy engine — Scramjet by default — and loads the page inside the interface, bypassing network restrictions transparently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Games & Apps">
    Use the navigation to visit the built-in content library. Head to `/a` for the **Games** page or `/b` for the **Apps** page. Every title loads directly through the proxy, so no extra setup or permissions are needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For the most browser-like experience, open the **Tab System** at `/d`. It gives you persistent tabs with full back, forward, and reload controls — ideal for keeping multiple proxied sites open at once.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Visit `/c` to open **Settings**, where you can switch proxy backends, apply custom themes, configure cloaking options (tab title, favicon, URL disguise), and set up your Panic Key.
</Note>
