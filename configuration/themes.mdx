Cloudy includes a set of built-in themes that let you customize the interface's color palette to suit your taste. Themes are applied instantly when you select them and saved to your browser — no page reload required.

## How to Apply a Theme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to `/c` or click the **Settings** icon in the Cloudy navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Themes card">
    Scroll to the **Themes!** card on the settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a theme">
    Open the dropdown and choose any theme from the list. The new color scheme is applied to the entire interface immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Catppuccin Themes

Catppuccin is a community-driven pastel color scheme available in four flavors, from deep dark to soft light.

<Note>
  Catppuccin is a popular open-source pastel color scheme project used across many apps and tools. Learn more at [catppuccin.com](https://catppuccin.com/).
</Note>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Mocha" icon="moon">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Macchiato" icon="star">

## Custom Background Image

You can further personalize Cloudy by setting your own background image, independent of the active theme.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to `/c`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Background Image card">
    Locate the **Set Background Image** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an image URL">
    Paste a direct link to your chosen image into the **Enter image URL** field, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset if needed">
    Click **Reset** on the same card to remove the custom background and return to the theme's default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a direct image link — one that ends in a file extension like `.jpg`, `.png`, `.gif`, or `.webp`. Links to pages that *display* an image (rather than linking to the image file itself) will not work as backgrounds.
</Tip>

## Particles

Cloudy includes an optional animated particle effect as a callback to earlier versions of the project.

- Open Settings (`/c`) and locate the **Particles** card.
- Flip the toggle **on** to enable the animated particles overlay.
- Flip it **off** to disable the effect.

The setting is saved immediately and persists across page loads.
