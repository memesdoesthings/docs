Cloudy proxies your web traffic through its own server, letting you reach websites that would otherwise be blocked on your network. Just type a URL or search term into the home page search bar and Cloudy handles the rest — no extensions, no extra installs required. The default proxy engine is **Scramjet**, which works out of the box for most sites.

## How to Browse

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Cloudy home page">
    Navigate to the root of your Cloudy instance (e.g. `https://your-cloudy-instance.com/`). You'll see the search bar in the centre of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a URL or search term">
    Type a full URL (e.g. `https://example.com`) or a plain search term into the bar and press **Enter**. Cloudy will route URLs directly through the proxy engine, and search terms through your configured search engine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse freely">
    The proxied page loads in your current tab. You can follow links, log in to sites, and generally browse as normal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing a Proxy Engine

Cloudy supports three proxy engines. You can switch between them at any time from Settings without losing your session.

| Engine | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Scramjet** (default) | Fastest and most compatible engine. Recommended for everyday browsing. |
| **Ultraviolet** (legacy) | An older but stable alternative. Useful if a specific site behaves better with it. |
| **Dynamic** | An alternative routing mode. Note that Dynamic **does not work** inside an About:Blank popup window. |

**To switch engines:**

1. Go to **Settings** (`/c`).
2. Find the **Choose your Proxy** card.
3. Select your preferred engine from the dropdown.
4. Your choice is saved automatically to your browser's local storage.

## Search Engine

When you type a plain search term (rather than a URL), Cloudy routes it through your configured search engine. The default is **Brave Search**.

**Available search engines:**

- Brave *(default)*
- Google
- Bing
- Qwant
- Startpage
- SearchEncrypt
- Ecosia

**To change your search engine:**

1. Go to **Settings** (`/c`).
2. Find the **Search Engine** card.
3. Select an engine from the dropdown — your choice saves immediately.
4. To use a custom engine not in the list, paste its search URL (including the `?q=` parameter) into the text field below the dropdown and click **Save**.

```text Example custom engine URL
https://duckduckgo.com/?q=
```

<Tip>
  For the best browsing experience, open sites inside the **Tab System** (`/d`). It gives you a full browser-like interface with navigation controls, multiple tabs, and DevTools — all without leaving Cloudy.
</Tip>
