Cloudy is a self-hostable web proxy platform built on the Interstellar/Scramjet stack. Whether you're on a school network, a workplace firewall, or any other restricted connection, Cloudy routes your traffic through its proxy engine so you can reach any site, play browser games, and run web apps — all from a clean, cloakable interface that looks nothing like a traditional proxy.
